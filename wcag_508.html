My testing work

I hold a DHS Trusted Tester certification. I’m building my experience in accessibility testing and exploring ways to help identify barriers in websites and digital content.

My sample evaluations will use the DHS Trusted Tester process and the Section 508 Compliance Test Report (SCRT). Each report will describe what was tested, when it was tested, the methods used, and the limits of the evaluation.

These will be personal sample evaluations. They will not be DHS reports, legal advice, or a guarantee that a website conforms to WCAG or Section 508.
