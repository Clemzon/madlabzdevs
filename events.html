Standards, laws, and court updates

DOJ extends ADA Title II web accessibility deadlines

A 2026 interim final rule extended the deadlines for state and local governments to meet the web and mobile app requirements based on WCAG 2.1 Level AA. Entities serving 50,000 or more people now have until April 26, 2027. Smaller entities and special district governments have until April 26, 2028.

This is an ADA Title II update for state and local governments. Section 508 is a separate law covering information and communication technology used by federal agencies. Read the Department of Justice rule .

Henry v. Omoi, Inc.: court declines to approve proposed consent judgment

In March 2026, a federal judge denied a request to turn the parties’ settlement in a website accessibility case into a court-enforceable consent judgment. The order focused on the proposed settlement’s terms. It did not decide the broader question of how the ADA applies to private websites.

Assistive technology and AI

Harvard researchers develop AI-powered navigation app

Mobilio uses a smartphone’s sensors, AI, and personalized audio cues to guide blind and low-vision users and help detect obstacles. In early tests, participants using Mobilio with a cane completed an outdoor route faster and had fewer obstacle contacts on an indoor course than when using Google Maps with a cane. Researchers plan further testing; Mobilio is not described as a released product.

Testing and research opportunities

These programs let people share feedback on websites, apps, and other digital products. Registration usually adds you to a pool; invitations depend on the needs of each study.

Join the Access Usability Project participant pool

The project matches participants with research studies on digital products, prototypes, websites, and apps. It is open to U.S. residents aged 18 or older who have a disability or impairment related to vision, hearing, speech, mobility, or thinking and learning. The project says participants are compensated for their time.
