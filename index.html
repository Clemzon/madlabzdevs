Building accessible ideas together

MadLabzDevs explores how universal design can make technology easier to access and use. We bring people together to discuss real-world barriers and work on practical ideas and tools that can help address them.

A user-informed approach

People who experience barriers in digital content and technology have valuable perspectives to share. Their experiences can help shape better questions, ideas, and solutions.

We keep universal design in focus. The goal is to make technology work for more people, without treating accessibility as something separate from the needs of everyone who uses it.

An idea taking shape

MadLabzDevs began with an idea: develop accessible technology shaped by real-world experience and designed to be useful to a broad range of people. The work is at an early stage, with more ideas and opportunities for collaboration ahead.

The approach is grounded in learning from people who encounter barriers in digital spaces and technology, then exploring practical solutions together. Universal design helps keep the focus on creating technology that works for more people.
