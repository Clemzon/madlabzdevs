Submission details
For a barrier report, you could describe what you were trying to do, what happened, and what access technology or approach you used. Please don’t include private information.
Your email would be used to contact you about your submission. It would not be displayed publicly unless you choose that option below.

Community guidelines

The community should make room for honest frustration and strong opinions while keeping discussion useful and respectful.

  • Describe the barrier, experience, or idea clearly.
  • Focus criticism on products, services, and design decisions, not personal attacks.
  • Do not post private, identifying, or sensitive information about yourself or others.
  • Share claims as your experience unless you can point to reliable supporting information.
  • Understand that submissions may be edited for clarity, privacy, or safety before publication.

A future version of this page will explain how submissions are reviewed, how long review may take, and how to request removal of a post.
